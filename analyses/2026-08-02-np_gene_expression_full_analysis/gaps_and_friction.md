# Gaps and friction

Transitional log of methodology / KG / tooling friction encountered during this analysis. Distinct from decisions (which live in each step's `notebook.md`). Append-only.

## 2026-08-02 — "nitrogen" treatment_type mixes starvation with alternate-N-source growth

While enumerating nitrogen experiments in step 2, `list_experiments(treatment_type=['nitrogen'], ...)` returned both true starvation/deprivation contrasts and "growth on alternate N source (cyanate/urea/nitrite) vs N-replete" contrasts under the same `treatment_type` tag. These are different biological questions (nutrient depletion vs. alternate-source utilization), but nothing in the KG schema (`treatment_type`, `background_factors`) distinguishes them — only the free-text `treatment`/`control` fields do. This mattered here because several of the analysis's nitrogen-annotated genes (`cynA/B/D/S`, `ureA–G`, `nirA`) are themselves alternate-N-source-utilization genes, so the excluded experiments were arguably the most on-target evidence for those specific genes. Worked around by manually reading `treatment`/`control` text per experiment and excluding by experiment ID (see `2_kg_selection/scripts/02_list_np_experiments.py`). Downstream impact: the analysis's nitrogen-side results for `cyn*`/`ure*`/`nirA` are conservative relative to what the KG actually contains — a caveat to carry to step 6. Phosphorus treatment_type had no equivalent split. Methodology takeaway: when a gene's annotated function is "use compound X as a nutrient source," treatment_type alone is not enough to tell whether an experiment is on-target — the treatment/control text must be read.

## 2026-08-02 — Strain scope (SS120) surfaced late, required a step-2 redo

The researcher's study excludes SS120 (CCMP1375), but this was not mentioned during step 1 or step 2's co-define — it only surfaced during step 3's co-define, after step 2 (experiment enumeration, gene-locus resolution) had already been committed with SS120 included. Handled via the redo path: step 2's script/data/notebook were regenerated without SS120 and recommitted as a new commit (prior commit `2da4c3b` preserved in history). Process takeaway: for analyses that build on a researcher's specific named strain/study set (as this one explicitly does, inheriting from the "15 strains 4 media" paper), confirm the exact strain list during step-1 co-define rather than assuming "all strains with data in the KG" is in scope.
